EV6 KOR wired Apple CarPlay TBT v0.1.6 source and local builder.
The signed mod archive is built from mod/. The signing key is external.

Upstream HMG-API v1.0.1 by VysseCodes, AGPL-3.0:
https://github.com/VysseCodes/HMG-API
IONIQ 5 external wireless CarPlay reference by b-lack-kr, AGPL-3.0:
https://github.com/b-lack-kr/ioniq5-external-wirelss-carplay

This mod is a derivative of HMG-API v1.0.1 and uses ideas from the IONIQ 5
project. Its CpTurns.java is based on the upstream HMG-API implementation.
The revised v0.1.4 adds angle and distance fallback, lane overwrite
protection, and owner-scoped display clearing to the subscription fixes.
Version 0.1.5 refreshes the displayed turn when late maneuver details arrive
and reports received messages separately by type.
Version 0.1.6 handles turns without an exit angle, preserves angles during
partial updates, and clears an obsolete arrow when the new step has no icon.
The added turn fallback and roundabout mappings reference Korea Projection's
HMG-API fork by StopDragon (AGPL-3.0), with ideas credited there to HMG-Seogaemo:
https://github.com/StopDragon/HMG-API/blob/38b4432/mods/wireless-carplay-aa/src/hmgapi/mods/wireless/dipo/CpTurns.java

Wired CarPlay turn updates and rerouting were verified on a Korean EV6
(CVEV KOR 260902). The project is provided without warranty under AGPL-3.0.
