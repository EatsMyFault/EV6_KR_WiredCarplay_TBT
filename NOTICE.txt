EV6 KOR wired Apple CarPlay TBT v0.1.3 experimental source and local builder.
The signed mod archive is built from mod/. The signing key is external.

Upstream HMG-API v1.0.1 by VysseCodes, AGPL-3.0:
https://github.com/VysseCodes/HMG-API
IONIQ 5 external wireless CarPlay reference by b-lack-kr, AGPL-3.0:
https://github.com/b-lack-kr/ioniq5-external-wirelss-carplay

This mod is a derivative of HMG-API v1.0.1 and uses ideas from the IONIQ 5
project. Its CpTurns.java is based on the upstream HMG-API implementation.
The v0.1.3 changes serialize route refresh with session events and handle
an empty maneuver list.

Vehicle operation, support, compatibility, and safety are unverified.
